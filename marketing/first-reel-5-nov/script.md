## Posting tips

- Add a trending Instagram track at ~10–15% volume (Instagram's own music keeps reach; the voice stays clear).
- Cover: pick the frame with "વેપારી: ભાવ ઓછો કરો!" (first 2 s).
- Put the Play Store link in bio; say "લિંક બાયોમાં" in a comment.
- Voice is Microsoft Edge neural TTS via the unofficial `edge-tts` tool – fine for a test reel; if ads/paid promotion grow, re-voice with Google Cloud TTS (gu-IN) or a real voice.
